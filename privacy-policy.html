Privacy Policy — Slant

Last updated: June 10, 2026

1. Overview

Slant operates an automated video content channel on TikTok and YouTube. This policy explains how we handle data.

2. Data We Collect

We do not collect personal data from viewers. The TikTok Developer API is used solely for publishing content to our own creator account.

3. TikTok API Usage

We use TikTok Content Posting API with the following scopes: video.upload (to upload video files) and video.publish (to publish videos to our TikTok account). We do not access any TikTok user data beyond our own account.

4. Cookies

Our integration does not use cookies or tracking pixels on viewer devices.

5. Third-Party Services

We use third-party services for audio and video production. No viewer data is shared with these services.
